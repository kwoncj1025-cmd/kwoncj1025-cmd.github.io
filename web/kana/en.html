Questions

What is Kana?

Kana is an anime character AI agent who lives on your Mac desktop. Hand her a task in a speech bubble and she runs mail, calendar, file and web errands through Claude Code, then reports back in one line. Before sending, paying or deleting anything, she always stops at an approval card. For macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later), one-time purchase.

Yes. AI usage is billed separately, to your own Anthropic account, not as part of Kana's price. Kana runs on an Anthropic API key (recommended) or a Claude Code sign-in.

How do I install it?

After checkout, download Kana-2026.11.27.dmg, open it, and drag Kana into your Applications folder. Enter your license key the first time you open it.

How many Macs can I use it on?

Three per key. When you change Macs, use “Remove key from this Mac” in Kana's settings to free the seat.

Where do my mail and files go?

Kana has no server of ours. Your request and the pieces it needs go straight from your Mac to your own Anthropic account. We cannot see them. Privacy policy

Does it run on Windows or Intel Macs?

A Windows version is on the way. We'll let you know when it's ready.

Does Kana speak English?

Yes. She speaks Korean and English.

Is Kana always right?

Kana runs on AI (Claude), so she can be wrong. She calculates with code and cites her sources, but please double-check important numbers and facts.
